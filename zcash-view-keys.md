# Zcash Viewing Keys

Viewing keys provide a powerful tool for transparency and compliance without compromising the security of your core funds.

## What is a Viewing Key?
A viewing key allows someone to view all incoming and outgoing transactions associated with a specific shielded address or pool, but it **cannot** be used to spend or transfer funds.

## Common Use Cases
* **Auditing and Taxes:** Easily share your financial history with accountants or tax software while keeping your spending keys safely locked away.
* **Compliance:** Demonstrating proof of funds or transaction history to trusted parties without exposing your entire wallet's master seed phrase.
