# Zcash Node Synchronization

Synchronizing a node or wallet with the Zcash network ensures that you have an up-to-date view of the blockchain and your balances.

## Full Nodes vs. Light Clients
* **Full Nodes:** Download and verify every block and transaction, providing maximum security and trustlessness.
* **Light Clients:** Rely on simplified payment verification (SPV) or compact block filters to sync quickly on mobile devices while maintaining privacy through shielded protocols.

## Best Practices for Syncing
* Ensure a stable internet connection during initial sync to prevent database corruption.
* Regularly update your node software to stay compatible with network upgrades and consensus rules.
