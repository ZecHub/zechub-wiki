# Start Here Path Wizard

Welcome to the ZecHub onboarding and navigation wizard! This guide is designed to help newcomers and contributors find their way through the ecosystem, documentation, and community resources efficiently.

## Core Navigation Paths

* **New to Zcash?** Begin by exploring the foundational concepts of privacy, cryptographic shielded pools, and decentralized currency basics.
* **Want to Contribute?** Check out our contribution guidelines, wiki style guide, and active GitHub repositories to start editing and writing documentation.
* **Looking for Tools?** Discover official wallets, block explorers, developer libraries, and community nodes.

## Community Resources

ZecHub is community-driven. You can connect with other contributors, ask questions, and join discussions across our active social channels and developer calls.
