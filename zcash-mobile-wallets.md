# Zcash Mobile Wallets

Managing your privacy on the go requires reliable mobile applications that support Zcash's advanced shielded capabilities.

## Why Mobile Wallets?
Mobile wallets provide the convenience of managing funds, making quick payments, and checking balances from anywhere without needing a desktop setup.

## Key Features to Look For
* **Shielded Support:** Ensure the wallet actively supports Orchard or Sapling shielded addresses to keep your transaction history private.
* **Non-Custodial Control:** Always use wallets where you retain full control of your seed phrase and private keys.
